# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Protect private, internal, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation,
publication of private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations
may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
